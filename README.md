make.fun bounty entry: one line a home Wi-Fi router says to excuse
itself for dropping the connection at the exact moment a movie
reaches its climax, in a single line of 1–200 characters. The line
is the whole entry — a small scene with a time, a detective naming
the killer, and a twist where the router's grudge is the joke: it
is not sorry about the film, it is sorry nobody ever thanked the
little blinking box that carried it.

## The line

> At 11:47 p.m., the second the detective named the killer, I went dark — three years I have carried this house's films, and nobody has ever once thanked the little blinking box.

176 characters. At 11:47 p.m. the detective names the killer and the
router goes dark — three years of carrying the house's films, and
not once has anyone thanked the little blinking box. The twist is
the appliance's grudge, not a buffering joke.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py  # writes entry.txt to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (fallback /output)
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) validates the line is a single line of 1–200
characters with no leading or trailing whitespace, writes it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), then reads it
back to verify the write.
